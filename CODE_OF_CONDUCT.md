# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and constructive
- Avoid harassment or discriminatory language
- Welcome newcomers and help them get started
- Report unacceptable behavior to the repo maintainers

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the repository maintainers.
